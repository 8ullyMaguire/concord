Why some projects say “not measured”

Health comes from the project's own repository — how recently it moved, how many people touch it, how fast a pull request gets an answer. A project whose repository we have not been told about cannot be measured, and this page says so rather than pretending it scored zero. Health is context, not a verdict: a small project with two commits a month can be the best place to start.
