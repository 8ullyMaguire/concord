Objections

    Close this call

    Closing fixes the result, and the result is written down. An open block is not something a close decides: §6.6's pass conditions end with “no unresolved block”, so a call with one standing closes blocked and the block stays on the record. Answering it is the override window's job.

    Override window

    §6.6's second window: a separate ballot on whether the block should stand. An override carries at 80% of non-abstain participants — everyone who took a position, reservations included — with the objector's remedy answered in writing.

    If this call stalls or is blocked

    §6.5: the ranking is the fallback chain, so this is where the call goes next — strongest first, with the baseline last. Adopting the next one down needs no second vote.

      Record the decision

      §6.5: the record carries the outcome, the positions, the objections, the remedies and the ranking it was decided against. It is written when you record one, and it cannot be recorded twice.

      The baseline is not in this list: rejecting the proposal is “Rejected”, which is §6.5's “the baseline wins”.
